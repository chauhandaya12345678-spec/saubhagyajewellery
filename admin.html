💡 How to name a product for Google (tap to open)
The website reads the Name you type and automatically writes the Google title, description, keywords and Shopping listing. So put the style words people actually search in the name.
Formula: [Style/Region] + [Material] + [Type]
✅ Kundan Bridal Choker Necklace    ✅ Punjabi Jhumki Earrings
✅ Meenakari Chandbali Earrings    ✅ Temple Lakshmi Long Haaram
✅ American Diamond Mangalsutra    ✅ Antique Oxidised Jhumka
❌ "Elegant Necklace" · "New Design 2" · "Beautiful Earring" — no style word = generic, weak ranking.
Words the engine recognises — use any that fit:
Material/style: Kundan, Polki, Jadau, Meenakari, Temple, Antique, Oxidised, American Diamond (AD), Crystal, Pearl
Region: Rajasthani, Punjabi, Bengali, Maharashtrian, Gujarati
Type: Jhumka, Jhumki, Chandbali, Stud, Drop, Choker, Rani Haar, Haaram, Mangalsutra, Pendant, Bangle, Maang Tikka, Nath, Anklet, Ring, Bridal Set
You don't need every word — 2 to 4 that describe the piece is perfect. Keep it readable, like a real product name.
