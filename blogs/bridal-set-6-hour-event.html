Choosing a bridal set that survives a 6-hour event

Weight decides comfort: pick a haaram that sits below the collarbone so it does not fight your dupatta pins, earrings with ear-chain support if they cross 40 grams, and a maang tikka with a hook — not a paste patch. Our made-to-order bridal sets are built exactly for this; message us on WhatsApp with your outfit photo and we will suggest the right set.

A long wedding day means sitting, standing, photos and blessings — every gram of jewellery gets heavier by hour four. Choose pieces with proper weight distribution: a choker with a balanced backing, earrings with chains that transfer weight to the ear rather than pulling the lobe, and a haaram that rests on the chest instead of hanging from the neck alone.
