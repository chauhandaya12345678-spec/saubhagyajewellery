7 Tips to Store & Care for Your Imitation Jewellery

Good gold-plated jewellery can last 3-5 years with proper care — or 3 months without it. The difference is almost entirely in how you store and clean your pieces. Here are seven science-backed tips that work.

1. Keep It Away From Water and Sweat

The fastest way to ruin gold plating is moisture. Always remove your jewellery before bathing, swimming or exercising. Sweat contains salt and acids that eat through plating over time. If a piece does get wet, pat it dry immediately with a soft cotton cloth — never use tissue paper, it leaves fibres in the settings.

2. Store Each Piece Separately

Metal-on-metal contact causes micro-scratches. Store each necklace, pair of earrings and set of bangles in its own soft pouch or compartment. A simple jewellery box with individual velvet-lined slots is worth every rupee. Never toss multiple pieces into one drawer.

3. Use Anti-Tarnish Strips

Those little black paper strips that come with new jewellery are not packaging filler — they are anti-tarnish strips that absorb sulphur and moisture from the air. Keep them in your jewellery box and replace them every 6 months. You can buy packs of 50 online for under ₹200.

4. The Plastic Bag Trick

For long-term storage, place each piece in a small zip-lock plastic bag and squeeze out as much air as possible before sealing. Less air means less oxidation. This is the same principle museums use to preserve metal artefacts.

5. Never Spray Perfume Directly on Jewellery

Perfume, deodorant and hairspray contain alcohol and chemicals that dull gold plating instantly. The golden rule: jewellery goes on last, after all your cosmetics are dry. If you wear a necklace with perfume on your neck, the plating will fade in that exact spot within weeks.

6. Clean Gently With a Dry Cloth

For everyday cleaning, a dry microfiber or soft cotton cloth is all you need. Rub gently in one direction — never scrub in circles. Avoid liquid jewellery cleaners; most contain ammonia which strips gold plating. If a piece needs deeper cleaning, use a slightly damp cloth with a single drop of mild soap, then dry thoroughly.

7. Rotate Your Collection

Wearing the same necklace every day wears it out faster. Rotate between 3-4 everyday pieces so each gets rest days. This is especially important for gold-plated chains which experience the most friction against skin and clothing.
