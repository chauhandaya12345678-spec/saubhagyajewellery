Handcrafted Indian jewellery for every region and every occasion

Saubhagya brings together the jewellery traditions of all of India in one place. From South Indian temple jewellery - Lakshmi and peacock motifs, antique gold-finish long haaram and short necklaces - to North Indian Kundan and Polki bridal sets, delicate Meenakari enamel work, sparkling American Diamond (AD/CZ) pieces, and everyday gold-plated designs, every style is handcrafted in our own Mumbai workshop and finished by hand.

Shop jhumka and jhumki earrings, chandbali, studs and drops; choker necklaces, rani haar, mangalsutra, maang tikka, nath and complete bridal jewellery sets. Whether you are dressing for a wedding, reception, engagement, Diwali, Navratri or Karva Chauth, a festive pooja or everyday office wear, there is a piece for the moment - Rajasthani, Punjabi, Bengali, Maharashtrian and Gujarati looks included.

Every order ships free and fully insured across India, is individually inspected and sealed before dispatch, and any manufacturing defect is repaired or replaced free. Explore the full collection above, or read our jewellery care & buying guide and FAQ before you order.

Necklaces

Two shapes, both antique gold. The short temple necklace sets sit high at the collar on a back tie-cord with matching Lakshmi jhumkas - mango links, peacock panels, kemp stone work, in gold, green or pearl drops. The long harams are five strands of faceted crystal beads under a Lakshmi pendant, in maroon or green.

Earrings

Two families. Kundan chandbali in six shapes - round, teardrop, fan and leaf - set with uncut-look stones and pearl drops. And jhumkas in two finishes: kundan-paved with a seed-pearl border, or plain deep-cut South Indian antique gold. Lightest pair is 27 g.
